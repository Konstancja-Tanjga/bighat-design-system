# Elevation & materials

New in 4.2. Until 4.1 every surface was drawn by a 1px border, and elevation
was a single 8%-opacity shadow that most components never used. The result read
as flat: a card, a menu and a dialog were the same white rectangle with the same
grey line around it, and the only thing telling them apart was where they sat.

4.2 draws surfaces with **height** instead of lines.

## The scale

Five steps. Each is two or three layered shadows - a tight contact shadow and a
wide ambient one - because a single layer reads either as a smudge or as a
border, never as height.

<ElevationScale />

`elevation.raised` is the default for any surface. Its first layer is a 1px ring
at 3.5% opacity: that ring is what replaced the border. It is there so a white
card on a white page still has an edge, and faint enough that it reads as the
card's shape rather than as a line.

In the dark theme a shadow on a near-black page is invisible, so height is
carried by light: `raised` and `floating` add a 1px highlight along the top
edge. Switch the theme in the toolbar to see it.

## Materials

Anything that floats **over** content is glass: a translucent fill, a backdrop
blur, and a saturation boost that keeps the colour behind it from going grey.

<MaterialScale />

A material is three declarations and a shadow:

```css
.my-popover {
  background: var(--bh-material-regular-bg);
  backdrop-filter: blur(var(--bh-material-regular-blur)) saturate(var(--bh-material-saturation));
  box-shadow: var(--bh-material-rim), var(--bh-elevation-overlay);
  border: 0;
}
```

`material.rim` is the lit inner edge of the glass and always goes first in the
shadow list. It replaces the border on every glass surface.

- `thin` - Tooltip, a control laid over an image
- `regular` - Menu, Combobox listbox, Toast
- `thick` - Dialog, SidePanel - the most text, so the most opaque
- `chrome` - AppBar and sticky headers, with content scrolling beneath

**Only over content.** On an empty page glass has nothing to blur, and a
translucent white over white is just a slightly grey rectangle. Cards in a grid
stay solid `surface.raised` with `elevation.raised`.

<TokenTable group="material" />

## Supporting roles

- `border.hairline` - the edge of something with no border: a row divider inside
  a card, the line under the app bar. Fainter than `border.subtle`, and like it,
  decorative only.
- `fill.hover` - a hovered row on glass. `surface.sunken` is opaque and punches a
  solid hole in the material.
- `scrim.*` - behind a Dialog. 18% instead of the old 55%, because the blur now
  does the separating and the page underneath can stay recognisable.

## What changed in the components

- **Card** - border, flat by default → no border, `raised` by default, hover lifts to `floating`
- **Dialog** - border, 55% scrim → `material.thick`, `radius.modal`, blurred 18% scrim
- **Menu, Combobox** - border, opaque → `material.regular`, `radius.overlay`
- **Toast** - border and a square tone stripe → `material.regular`, tone stripe follows the corner
- **AppBar** - `surface.base` → `material.chrome` with a hairline

`radius.surface` also grew from 10px to 20px: with no border to draw it, the
corner is what gives a surface its shape. Every component on `radius.surface`
picks this up.
