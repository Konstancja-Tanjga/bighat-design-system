## Props

| prop | values | default | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `brand` | slot | — | Logo or wordmark. |
| `title` | slot | — | Names the current screen, not the product. |
| `titleAsHeading` | boolean | `true` | false when the screen has its own visible <h1>. |
| `center` | slot | — | Search or filters acting on this screen. |
| `actions` | slot | — | Primary action last. |
