## Props

| prop | values | default | meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `header` | slot | — | Rendered as `<header>`. |
| `rail` | slot | — | Narrow icon rail on the leading edge. |
| `sidebar` | slot | — | Leading panel — navigation, a tree. |
| `aside` | slot | — | Trailing panel — inspector, context. |
| `children` | slot | — | Becomes the only `<main>`. |
| `height` | `fill` · `flow` | `fill` | fill pins to the viewport and scrolls each region independently |
| `navOpen` | boolean | `false` | Below breakpoint.md the rail and sidebar leave the grid; with onNavToggle they return as an overlay instead of simply vanishing. |
| `onNavToggle` | event | — | Omit and the panels stay hidden below the breakpoint — honest, but the reader has no route back to them. |
